About
LoHo Learning

LoHo Learning is a Kenyan EdTech company on a mission to make education accessible, engaging, and inclusive for every child across Africa. We build connected classrooms and digital tools that bring learning to life.

From curriculum-aligned digital content to school management tools, we partner with institutions, parents, and communities to close the education gap — one learner at a time.

Our Solutions

Our featured K–12 platform and full suite of digital education tools, built for every learner in Africa.

Featured Platform

Elimu Pepe Kenya

Kenya's leading K–12 digital learning platform, delivering CBC-aligned lessons, assessments, and interactive content for learners from Grade 1 to Form 4. Designed to work offline and on low-end devices.

  • CBC & 8-4-4 Curriculum
  • Works Offline on any Device
  • Live Teacher-Student Interaction
Explore Platform
Campus Management

Loho Campus

LoHo Campus is a digital learning ecosystem by LoHo Learning, bringing together curriculum-aligned content, interactive eBooks, eLearning tools, and accessibility solutions in one platform. It empowers learners, educators, and institutions with engaging, inclusive, and locally relevant digital education—anytime, anywhere.

  • Digital School Administration
  • Learner-Teacher Communication
  • Academic Reporting & Analytics

Accessibility & Inclusion

eSharah

eSharah is LoHo Learning’s accessibility and inclusion suite — empowering learners with disabilities through inclusive digital education, assistive learning tools, and specialized support for diverse learning needs.

  • Kenya Sign Language (KSL) & Digital Braille Learning
  • Inclusive Tools for Deaf, Blind & Visually Impaired Learners
  • Autism Support & Emotional Learning Solutions

Tech & Coding

Qureo Coding

Qureo Coding is LoHo Learning’s AI-powered coding platform that equips children with computational thinking and programming skills through gamified lessons, interactive eBooks, and hands-on digital learning experiences.

  • Scratch & Java Programming for Young Learners
  • Gamified Coding Lessons & Interactive eBooks
  • Computational Thinking & Digital Skills Development
